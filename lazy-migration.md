# Cataloguing a Day of Fun - My experience upgrading to lazy.nvim

Let me start by saying - this is 100% an upgrade. My experience was
starting to get slow between lsp-zero and a couple of other heavyweight plugins.

With Lazy, load times have gone way down, my config feels simpler/I understand it better.

10/10 would recommend the upgrade
